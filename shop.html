Five Latin dishes, ready in minutes.

Every dish is cooked by Latin cooks in a licensed kitchen, sealed in its own tray and kept in the fridge. Here is what is inside each one, how to heat it, and where it comes from.

01 · Chile

Empanada de Pino

Chilean beef empanadas · 2 pack

Chile's favourite empanada: beef, onion, egg and olive, baked until golden.

  • Baked, not fried
  • Hand-chopped beef
  • Microwave 2 min
Inside
2 empanadas × 165 g (330 g)
Protein*
~38 g per pack
Energy*
~920 kcal per pack
How to heat
Microwave 1 min 30 s, then rest 1 min.
The tray
A low rectangular tray: the two empanadas lie side by side, so they arrive whole.
Ingredients (draft)
Wheat flour, beef (35%), onion, egg, lard, butter, milk, black olives, raisins, paprika, cumin, oregano, salt.
Allergens (draft)
Wheat, Gluten, Egg, Milk.

Empanada de Lomo Saltado

Peruvian stir-fried beef empanadas · 2 pack

Peru's wok-tossed beef classic, folded into a baked empanada.

  • Wok-tossed beef
  • Baked, not fried
  • Microwave 2 min
Inside
2 empanadas × 165 g (330 g)
Protein*
~34 g per pack
Energy*
~780 kcal per pack

Microwave 1 min 30 s, then rest 1 min.
The tray
Same tray as the pino, with a divider: each empanada keeps its own place.

Wheat flour, beef (30%), red onion, tomato, potato, soy sauce, yellow chilli paste, lard, butter, milk, egg, coriander, garlic, cumin, vinegar.

03 · Chile

Pastel de Choclo

Chilean sweet corn & beef bake

A Chilean summer bake: savoury beef and chicken underneath, a sweet corn crust on top.

  • Two meats
  • Sweet corn crust
  • Microwave 4 min
Inside
1 portion · 400 g
Protein*
~40 g per pack
Energy*
~600 kcal per pack
How to heat
Microwave 4 min, then rest 1 min.
The tray
Round and deep like a clay pot, so the layers stay apart and it heats evenly.
Ingredients (draft)
Sweet corn, beef (21%), chicken (12%), onion, milk, butter, egg, black olives, basil, sugar, wheat flour, cumin, paprika, salt.
Allergens (draft)
Milk, Egg, Wheat, Gluten.

04 · Venezuela

Pabellón Criollo

Shredded beef, black beans, rice & plantain

Venezuela's national plate: shredded beef, black beans, white rice and sweet plantain.

  • Beef + black beans
  • Four parts, kept apart
  • Microwave 4 min
Inside
1 plate · 450 g, in 4 parts
Protein*
~50 g per pack
Energy*
~900 kcal per pack
How to heat
Microwave 3–4 min, then rest 1 min.
The tray
Four compartments, served the way it is in Venezuela: the rice never gets soggy.
Ingredients (draft)
Rice, beef (31%), black beans, plantain, tomato, onion, capsicum, sunflower oil, garlic, brown sugar, cumin, annatto, salt.
Allergens (draft)
None of the major allergens.

05 · Argentina

Asado Mixto

Mixed grill, rice & salsa criolla + chimichurri

An Argentine Sunday asado: three meats off the grill, with chimichurri on the side.

  • 3 meats: beef, chorizo, chicken
  • Chimichurri made in-house
  • Microwave 4 min
Inside
1 plate · 465 g + 30 ml chimichurri
Protein*
~65 g per pack
Energy*
~950 kcal per pack
How to heat
Take out the chimichurri pot. Microwave 3–4 min, then rest 1 min.
The tray
The big compartment holds the three meats; rice and salsa criolla sit beside them. The chimichurri travels apart because it is not heated.
Ingredients (draft)
Beef flank (36%), chicken thigh (26%), chorizo (pork, beef, salt, spices), rice, tomato, red onion, capsicum, vinegar, butter, parsley, sunflower oil, garlic, oregano, chilli.
Allergens (draft)
Milk.
