How it works for cooks

01
You cook

Your recipes or ours, in a licensed kitchen, on your own schedule.

02
We sell

On Uber Eats and DoorDash, as part of the SolubFood menu.

03
We tell your story

Photos and video by our team, for SolubFood and for your own socials.

04
You get paid weekly

A share of every dish you cooked that sells, paid every week with a report of what sold.

Cooks Rights

  • You decide how, where and when you cook and film.
  • Your own kitchen, your own tools, your own phone.
  • Keep selling on your own socials and to anyone else. We're an extra channel, not a replacement.
  • No boss and no fixed hours. Say no to an order, no penalty.
  • Paid on time, with a weekly report of what sold.
  • Your name, face and story are used only with your signed consent.
  • Leave whenever you like, with two weeks' notice.
  • We never ask you to work more hours than your visa allows.
  • Every dish comes from a licensed kitchen and is labelled to Australian food standards.
  • We name the country and the people, never a stereotype.
