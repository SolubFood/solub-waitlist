Questions

Can I really cancel anytime?

Yes, in one tap. Cancel before Sunday 8 pm and you won't be charged again. If you cancel after, that week's pack is already paid and being cooked for you, so it still arrives on Thursday.

Can I skip a week?

Yes. Skip before Sunday 8 pm and nothing is charged that week. Skipping doesn't reset your price.

What's the Founding 30?

The first 30 members get their first Trial delivery for A$45. After that it's the normal plan price.

Where do you deliver?

Southport, Surfers Paradise, Bundall, Broadbeach, Main Beach, Benowa, Ashmore, Labrador, Mermaid Beach, Nerang and Robina. Thursday, 5–8 pm.

How do I heat it?

Straight from the freezer in the microwave, in its tray. The exact time is on every label.

Allergens?

Empanadas de pino: wheat, gluten, egg, milk. Pastel de choclo: milk, egg, wheat, gluten. Asado mixto: milk. Full ingredients on every label.
