Our vision

A home, far from home.

SolubFood exists so Australians can live Latin culture as it really is (visible, trusted, modern and built by its community, not a stereotype) and so Latin Americans on the Gold Coast feel at home.

HOGAR

Home

The flavours and rituals that make you feel you never left.

INDEPENDENCIA

Independence

Our cooks and partners keep their own name, their own story and their own way of doing things.

FAMILIARIDAD

Familiarity

Twenty countries, one community. Different accents, the same table.

SIMPLICIDAD

Simplicity

Easy to find, easy to buy, easy to share. No passport needed.

El Kiosko by SolubFood · Coming to the Gold Coast

When Gold Coast closes, Latin America opens.

In Argentina, the kiosko is the corner shop that never seems to close: a snack, a cold drink, a phone charger, whatever the night needs. We're bringing it to the Gold Coast, with home-style Latin food cooked by Latin cooks who live here.

Questions.

What is SolubFood?

A Latin brand from the Gold Coast. We share the food and culture of Latin America, and we're building El Kiosko, a late-night spot for home-style Latin food.

What is El Kiosko?

A Latin corner shop that stays open late: home-style Latin food from local Latin cooks, plus drinks, snacks and the everyday things you need at night. Order on your delivery app or walk in.

When and where will it open?

We're planning it now. There's no date or address yet. Members help choose the area and hear first.

Who cooks the food?

Latin cooks who live on the Gold Coast. Every dish carries the name of the person who made it. Meet them in the Kitchen.

I cook Latin food. Can I join?

Yes. Tell us about your food on the Kitchen page. Every dish has to come from a licensed kitchen and be labelled to Australian food standards.

How much will it cost?

Prices come with the opening. Members see them first.
