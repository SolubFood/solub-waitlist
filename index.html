Our Vision

A home, far from home.

SolubFood exists so Australians can live Latin culture as it really is (visible, trusted, modern and built by its community, not a stereotype) and so Latin Americans on the Gold Coast feel at home.

Draft · provisional text

SolubFood started with a simple feeling: that the food and rituals of home can travel. One ocean sits between Latin America and the Gold Coast, and we want to make it feel smaller.

We do it with home-style food cooked by people with names and stories, with films made where that culture is lived, and with nights around the same table. Real over perfect.

“We don't just sell food. We sell a feeling from the other side of the world.”

Benjamin Arevalo & Gibril
Founder & co-founder (marketing) · Gold Coast · [Gibril's photo goes here]

Questions.

What is SolubFood?

A Latin brand from the Gold Coast. We share the food and culture of Latin America: home-style food from local Latin cooks, stories filmed where they're lived, and Latin nights.

Who cooks the food?

Latin cooks who live on the Gold Coast. Every dish carries the name of the person who made it. Meet them in the Kitchen.

When do you start?

We're planning to start in 2027 on the Gold Coast. Members hear first, and the first 250 taste the food first.

I cook Latin food. Can I join?

Yes. Tell us about your food in Join Us. Every dish has to come from a licensed kitchen and be labelled to Australian food standards.

How much will it cost?

Prices come with the launch. Members see them first.

Is joining the waitlist free?

Yes. It's just your name and email, and you can leave any time.
