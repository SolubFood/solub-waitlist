That's the gap Solub Food exists to close.

You know the feeling.

You've smelled something incredible coming from a house on your street. You've scrolled through the same delivery apps and found the same handful of restaurants. The food you're actually craving — someone's abuela's arepas, a family recipe nobody ever wrote down — isn't on any of them.

That's not a hygiene problem, or a quality problem.
It's a discovery problem.

Solub Food exists to close that gap — putting a name, a story, and a simple way to order in front of the home kitchens that were always near you, just never visible.

Where we're at

Solub Food is in its earliest days — we're building the founding chef network from the ground up, starting here in Queensland. Profile information is reviewed before anyone goes live, and council registration is noted where applicable. We'll never claim more certainty than we have.

What we can promise: you'll always know who made your food, and why.
