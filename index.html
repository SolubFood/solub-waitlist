Our vision

A home, far from home.

SolubFood exists so Latin Americans on the Gold Coast feel at home, and so Australians can see Latin culture as it really is: visible, trusted, modern and built by its community. Not a stereotype.

HOGAR

Home

The flavours and rituals that make you feel you never left.

INDEPENDENCIA

Independence

Producers and partners keep their own name, their own story and their own way of doing things.

FAMILIARIDAD

Familiarity

Twenty countries, one community. Different accents, the same table.

SIMPLICIDAD

Simplicity

Easy to find, easy to buy, easy to share. No passport needed.
