Who cooks your food

Every SolubFood meal is cooked by our own cooks — the same people, every week — in a licensed commercial kitchen in Gold Coast. They work inside a process we designed from scratch, so that every plate comes out professional, and exactly the same, every time.

👩‍🍳 The same cooks, every week

Not a rotating line. Our cooks know each recipe by heart — that's what keeps the taste consistent.

⚖️ Recipes to the gram

Every recipe is weighed, timed and written step by step. The hundredth empanada tastes like the first.

🌡️ Every batch checked

Cooked to 75 °C in the centre, cooled within food-safety times and frozen at −18 °C — logged batch by batch. Each label carries its batch code and the date it was made.

❄️ Cooked to order, frozen the same day

We only cook what's been ordered, and freeze it the day it's made. Nothing sits on a shelf waiting.

Questions

Where do you deliver?

Gold Coast.

What if I'm not home?

Tell us when you check out and we'll leave it in an insulated bag, or message us on WhatsApp to arrange pick-up.

How do I heat it?

Microwave, straight in its tray — the exact time is on every label. Keep frozen at −18 °C; once thawed, eat within 1–2 days.

Allergens?

Empanadas de pino: wheat, gluten, egg, milk. Pastel de choclo: milk, egg, wheat, gluten. Asado mixto: milk. Full ingredients are on every label.

Can I cancel or get a refund?

You can cancel for a full refund until the order cut-off (Sunday 8 pm). After that we've already bought the ingredients for you — but if anything is wrong with your order, we'll make it right.
