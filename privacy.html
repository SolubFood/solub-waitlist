Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

Solub Food ("we", "us") is a guide and community about Latin American food and culture, launching in the Gold Coast, Queensland. The website is operated by [LEGAL NAME OF OPERATOR — e.g. "[Full name] trading as Solub Food" or "Solub Food Pty Ltd"], ABN [ABN]. Contact: hello@teamsolub.com.

We're a guide only. We don't prepare, sell or deliver food, and we don't process payments for food.

We aim to handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

2. What we collect today

Solub Food is in its earliest days: there are no user accounts yet, and no live directory of restaurants. Here's what we actually collect, through the forms on this site:

  • Join the waitlist — your name and suburb/postcode.
  • Become a founding chef — your name, phone or Instagram handle, and the dishes you cook.
  • Newsletter signup — your email address.
  • Anything you tell us directly, if you email us.

We don't collect payment details, health or allergy information, identity documents, or date of birth through this site.

3. Why we collect it

  • To let you know when Solub Food launches and keep you updated on our progress.
  • To review founding-chef applications.
  • To reply if you contact us.

4. How your information is submitted

Our forms are processed by formsubmit.co, a third-party form-delivery service, which forwards your submission to our inbox (hello@teamsolub.com). [CONFIRM: formsubmit.co's data handling and storage location — see their own privacy policy]. We don't run our own server or database at this stage.

5. Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We don't share it with any restaurant or third party for marketing purposes. It's used only by the Solub Food team.

6. Where it's stored

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., based in the United States). Form submissions pass through formsubmit.co [CONFIRM: location]. As we build real accounts and a live restaurant marketplace, this section will be updated to name our database and authentication provider and where they store data.

7. How long we keep it

We keep waitlist and founding-chef application details until launch, so we can follow up with you, or until you ask us to delete them.

8. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information anytime by emailing hello@teamsolub.com. We'll respond within a reasonable time — normally 30 days.

9. Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies, and we don't run analytics tools at this stage.

10. Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact us at hello@teamsolub.com. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au.

11. Changes

As Solub Food grows — user accounts, a live restaurant directory and community features are all on our roadmap — this policy will be updated to reflect what we actually do, with a new date and version number.
