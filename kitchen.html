Kitchen · The menu

Five Latin dishes, ready in minutes.

Cooked by Latin home cooks in a licensed kitchen on the Gold Coast, sealed in their own tray and kept in the fridge. Here is what is inside each dish, how to heat it and what it costs.

01 · Chile

Empanada de Pino

Chilean beef empanadas · 2 pack

Chile's favourite empanada: beef, onion, egg and olive, baked until golden.

  • Baked, not fried
  • Hand-chopped beef
  • Microwave 2 min
Inside
2 empanadas × 165 g (330 g)
Protein*
~38 g per pack
Energy*
~920 kcal per pack
How to heat
Microwave 1 min 30 s, then rest 1 min.
The tray
A low rectangular tray: the two empanadas lie side by side, so they arrive whole.
Ingredients & allergens (draft)

Ingredients: Wheat flour, beef (35%), onion, egg, lard, butter, milk, black olives, raisins, paprika, cumin, oregano, salt.

Allergens: Wheat, Gluten, Egg, Milk.

02 · Perú

Empanada de Lomo Saltado

Peruvian stir-fried beef empanadas · 2 pack

Peru's wok-tossed beef classic, folded into a baked empanada.

  • Wok-tossed beef
  • Baked, not fried
  • Microwave 2 min
Inside
2 empanadas × 165 g (330 g)
Protein*
~34 g per pack
Energy*
~780 kcal per pack
How to heat
Microwave 1 min 30 s, then rest 1 min.
The tray
Same tray as the pino, with a divider: each empanada keeps its own place.
Ingredients & allergens (draft)

Ingredients: Wheat flour, beef (30%), red onion, tomato, potato, soy sauce, yellow chilli paste, lard, butter, milk, egg, coriander, garlic, cumin, vinegar.

Allergens: Wheat, Gluten, Soy, Egg, Milk.

03 · Chile

Pastel de Choclo

Chilean sweet corn & beef bake

A Chilean summer bake: savoury beef and chicken underneath, a sweet corn crust on top.

  • Two meats
  • Sweet corn crust
  • Microwave 4 min
Inside
1 portion · 400 g
Protein*
~40 g per pack
Energy*
~600 kcal per pack
How to heat
Microwave 4 min, then rest 1 min.
The tray
Round and deep like a clay pot, so the layers stay apart and it heats evenly.
Ingredients & allergens (draft)

Ingredients: Sweet corn, beef (21%), chicken (12%), onion, milk, butter, egg, black olives, basil, sugar, wheat flour, cumin, paprika, salt.

Allergens: Milk, Egg, Wheat, Gluten.

04 · Venezuela

Pabellón Criollo

Shredded beef, black beans, rice & plantain

Venezuela's national plate: shredded beef, black beans, white rice and sweet plantain.

  • Beef + black beans
  • Four parts, kept apart
  • Microwave 4 min
Inside
1 plate · 450 g, in 4 parts
Protein*
~50 g per pack
Energy*
~900 kcal per pack
How to heat
Microwave 3–4 min, then rest 1 min.
The tray
Four compartments, served the way it is in Venezuela: the rice never gets soggy.
Ingredients & allergens (draft)

Ingredients: Rice, beef (31%), black beans, plantain, tomato, onion, capsicum, sunflower oil, garlic, brown sugar, cumin, annatto, salt.

Allergens: None of the major allergens.

~65 g protein*
05 · Argentina

Asado Mixto

Mixed grill, rice & salsa criolla + chimichurri

An Argentine Sunday asado: three meats off the grill, with chimichurri on the side.

  • 3 meats: beef, chorizo, chicken
  • Chimichurri made in-house
  • Microwave 4 min
Inside
1 plate · 465 g + 30 ml chimichurri
Protein*
~65 g per pack
Energy*
~950 kcal per pack
How to heat
Take out the chimichurri pot. Microwave 3–4 min, then rest 1 min.
The tray
The big compartment holds the three meats; rice and salsa criolla sit beside them. The chimichurri travels apart because it is not heated.
Ingredients & allergens (draft)

Ingredients: Beef flank (36%), chicken thigh (26%), chorizo (pork, beef, salt, spices), rice, tomato, red onion, capsicum, vinegar, butter, parsley, sunflower oil, garlic, oregano, chilli.

Allergens: Milk.
